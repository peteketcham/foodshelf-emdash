---
title: If You Need Help Right Now
description: Find food resources near Minnehaha Food Shelf in south Minneapolis, including United Way 2-1-1 and nearby grab-and-go meals.
---
United Way 2-1-1 provides free and confidential information about where to get food, clothing, medical care, legal assistance and other resources. ***If you need help right now, call 2-1-1*** (or toll-free 800-543-7709). Call centers are staffed 24 hours a day, seven days a week, or go to [211UNITEDWAY.ORG](https://211unitedway.org/). Other nearby sources of food include:

- **[HOPE PRESBYTERIAN CHURCH](https://www.hope-pc.org/)** offers grab-and-go meals every weekday from 5:00 to 6:00 p.m. (7132 Portland Av. S., Richfield).
- **[NOKOMIS HEIGHTS LUTHERAN CHURCH](https://nokomisheights.org/)** offers grab-and-go meals every Wednesday from 5:00 to 6:00 p.m. (5300 10th Av. S., Minneapolis).
- **[MOUNT OLIVET LUTHERAN CHURCH](https://mtolivet.org/)** offers grab-and-go meals every Thursday from 4:00 to 5:00 p.m. (5025 Knox Av. S., Minneapolis).
- **[SOUTH MINNEAPOLIS SALVATION ARMY](https://centralusa.salvationarmy.org/usc/)** serves a free hot lunch every Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday from Noon to 1:00 p.m. (1604 E. Lake St., Minneapolis)
- **[FIRST NATIONS KITCHEN](https://firstnationskitchen.org/)** distributes grab-and-go meals and produce every Sunday from 4:00 to 5:00 p.m. (3044 Longfellow Av. S., Minneapolis).
- **[MINNEHAHA UNITED METHODIST CHURCH](https://minnehaha.org/foodministries.html)** distributes produce and other groceries every fourth Saturday from 9:00 to 10:00 a.m. through its MinneHarvest program. (3701 E. 50th St., Minneapolis).
