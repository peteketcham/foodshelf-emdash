Emergency food assistance programs like ours are experiencing unprecedented and continuing surge in demand. In just the past few years, the number of households we serve weekly has grown dramatically — and it's still climbing. Meeting that need means we need more volunteers.

We need help with unloading trucks, food distribution setup and takedown, inventory management, registering and checking in clients, Spanish-English interpretation, community outreach, volunteer coordination, social media support, and other vital tasks. Many roles require being on-site during our open hours (Tuesdays, 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.), but others don't.

## Ready to get started? Here's how:

**New Volunteers:** Start with our monthly Volunteer Orientation — a site tour and onboarding session where you'll meet our team, see how we operate, and learn how to sign up for shifts. No experience necessary!

[**Click Here to Sign Up For Orientation**](https://www.signupgenius.com/go/60B0944A9AB22A4F49-64861005-volunteer#/)

**Looking for a flexible way to help?** Join us Monday mornings between 6:30 and 10:00 for staging and receiving!

[**Click Here to Sign Up Monday Shift**](https://www.signupgenius.com/go/60B0944A9AB22A4F49-63947907-minnehaha#/)

***A note from our manager***

> "Our volunteers are the heart of Minnehaha Food Shelf. As the need in our community keeps growing, so does our reliance on people willing to show up — whether that's once a month or every single week. Every hour you give means a neighbor experiencing food insecurity gets to bring home groceries to feed their family. I'd love for you to join us!"
>
> — Britt, Food Shelf Manager

Questions, or want more info before signing up? Email us at: [foodshelfvolunteers@minnehaha.org](mailto:foodshelfvolunteers@minnehaha.org).
